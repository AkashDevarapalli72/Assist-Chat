Policy 306 — Vehicle Pursuits

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2021-03-01. Revision 1. SUPERSEDED on 2024-01-15.

306.1 Authorization

Vehicle pursuits are authorized only when the suspect is wanted for any criminal offense. Officers shall weigh the danger of the pursuit against the need for immediate apprehension. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

306.2 Number of Pursuit Units

No more than five units may actively engage in a pursuit unless the supervisor authorizes additional vehicles. Additional units shall remain on parallel streets only when approved. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

306.3 Pursuit Termination

The supervisor shall order a pursuit terminated when speeds exceed 20 mph over the posted limit in a residential zone. A terminated pursuit must be acknowledged on the radio by every involved unit. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
