Policy 330 — Foot Pursuits

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

330.1 Pursuing Alone

Officers shall not initiate a foot pursuit alone unless a violent felony suspect is fleeing. Officers should consider containment and waiting for backup. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

330.2 Radio Broadcast

Officers engaged in a foot pursuit shall broadcast their location, direction of travel, and a suspect description. Radio traffic is kept clear for the pursuing officer. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
