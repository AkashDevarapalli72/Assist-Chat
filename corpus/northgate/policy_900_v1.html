Policy 900 — Public Records Requests

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2022-05-01. Revision 1. Current revision.

900.1 Response Time

Public records requests are fulfilled within 10 business days of receipt. Requests are logged by the records unit and acknowledged in writing. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

900.2 Copy Fees

Copies of public records are charged at $0.10 per page. Fees are waived for requests made by the subject of the record. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
