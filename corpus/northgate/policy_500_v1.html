Policy 500 — Missing Persons

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2022-05-01. Revision 1. Current revision.

500.1 Report Intake

Reports of missing persons are accepted with no waiting period. The responding officer shall obtain a recent photograph and a physical description. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

500.2 Supervisor Notification

A supervisor shall be notified within 30 minutes of any high-risk missing person report. High-risk includes children, endangered adults, and cases with suspected foul play. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.
