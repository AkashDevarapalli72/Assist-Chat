Policy 700 — Report Writing

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2022-05-01. Revision 1. Current revision.

700.1 Report Deadlines

Incident reports must be completed before the end of the shift. Reports shall be factual, objective, and written in the first person. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.

700.2 Report Approval

Supervisors approve or return incident reports within two business days. Returned reports shall be corrected and resubmitted promptly. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.
