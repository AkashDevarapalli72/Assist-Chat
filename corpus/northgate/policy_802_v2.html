Policy 802 — Property and Evidence

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2024-01-15. Revision 2. Current revision.

802.1 Packaging

Evidence shall be packaged in tamper-evident paper bags and sealed with the collecting officer's initials and date. Biological evidence must be dried before packaging. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.

802.2 Submission Deadline

All collected evidence must be submitted to the property room within 6 hours of collection. Items that cannot be submitted in time must be secured in a temporary locker. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.

802.3 Chain of Custody

Each transfer of evidence is recorded on the Property Transfer Log. Any gap in the record shall be reported to the property supervisor immediately. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.
