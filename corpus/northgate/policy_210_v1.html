Policy 210 — Emergency Vehicle Operation

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

210.1 Intersections

Officers operating in emergency mode shall stop at red signals and stop signs in all cases before proceeding. Emergency lights and siren must be activated for the duration of the response. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.
