Policy 308 — Conducted Energy Weapons

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2021-03-01. Revision 1. SUPERSEDED on 2024-01-15.

308.1 Deployment Criteria

Conducted energy weapons (commonly called Tasers) may be deployed only against a person who is actively resisting or who poses an immediate threat of harm. Deployment against a person who is only fleeing is never permitted. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

308.2 Cycle Limits

Officers shall not deliver more than ten cycles on a single subject without reassessing whether the weapon is effective. Each cycle must be documented in the use of force report. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.

308.3 Medical Evaluation

Any person struck by a conducted energy weapon probe shall be evaluated by EMS personnel before being booked. Probes embedded in sensitive areas are removed only by medical personnel. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.
