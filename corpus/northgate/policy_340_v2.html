Policy 340 — Body-Worn Cameras

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2024-01-15. Revision 2. Current revision.

340.1 Activation

Officers shall activate their body-worn cameras at dispatch to any call for service. Cameras remain on until the contact has concluded. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.

340.2 Retention

Routine recordings are retained for 90 days. Recordings that document a use of force or an arrest are flagged and retained under the evidence retention schedule. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.

340.3 Review Before Reporting

Officers may review their own recordings before writing a report, except following an officer-involved shooting, where review is allowed only after the investigator's approval. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.
