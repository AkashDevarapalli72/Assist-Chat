Policy 420 — Crisis Response

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2022-05-01. Revision 1. Current revision.

420.1 Crisis Intervention

Officers who encounter a person in mental health crisis shall request a Crisis Intervention Team officer when one is available. Officers should use de-escalation and allow time before taking enforcement action. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

420.2 Involuntary Holds

An officer may initiate an involuntary evaluation hold lasting up to 72 hours when the person is an imminent danger to self or others. The hold paperwork must accompany the person to the facility. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
