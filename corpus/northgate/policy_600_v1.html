Policy 600 — Domestic Violence

Northgate Police Department. Effective 2022-05-01. Revision 1. Current revision.

600.1 Arrest Decisions

In domestic violence incidents officers shall use mandatory arrest when probable cause exists. The decision shall be documented regardless of whether an arrest is made. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

600.2 Supplemental Report

A domestic violence supplemental report is due within 48 hours. Officers shall also provide the victim with a resource card. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
