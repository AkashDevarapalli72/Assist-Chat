Policy 600 — Domestic Violence

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2023-02-10. Revision 1. Current revision.

600.1 Arrest Decisions

In domestic violence incidents officers shall use a preferred-arrest approach. The decision shall be documented regardless of whether an arrest is made. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

600.2 Supplemental Report

A domestic violence supplemental report is due within 72 hours. Officers shall also provide the victim with a resource card. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
