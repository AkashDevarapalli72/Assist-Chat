Policy 330 — Foot Pursuits

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2025-01-06. Revision 1. Current revision.

330.1 Pursuing Alone

Officers shall not initiate a foot pursuit alone unless exigent circumstances exist. Officers should consider containment and waiting for backup. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

330.2 Radio Broadcast

Officers engaged in a foot pursuit shall broadcast their location, direction of travel, and the suspect's weapon status. Radio traffic is kept clear for the pursuing officer. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
