Policy 306 — Vehicle Pursuits

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2021-06-15. Revision 1. SUPERSEDED on 2024-03-01.

306.1 Authorization

Vehicle pursuits are authorized only when the suspect is wanted for any felony or serious misdemeanor. Officers shall weigh the danger of the pursuit against the need for immediate apprehension. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

306.2 Number of Pursuit Units

No more than six units may actively engage in a pursuit unless the supervisor authorizes additional vehicles. Additional units shall remain on parallel streets only when approved. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

306.3 Pursuit Termination

The supervisor shall order a pursuit terminated when speeds exceed 15 mph over the posted limit in a residential zone. A terminated pursuit must be acknowledged on the radio by every involved unit. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.
