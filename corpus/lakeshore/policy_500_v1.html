Policy 500 — Missing Persons

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2023-02-10. Revision 1. Current revision.

500.1 Report Intake

Reports of missing persons are accepted with a waiting period only for adults not considered endangered. The responding officer shall obtain a recent photograph and a physical description. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.

500.2 Supervisor Notification

A supervisor shall be notified within one hour of any high-risk missing person report. High-risk includes children, endangered adults, and cases with suspected foul play. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.
