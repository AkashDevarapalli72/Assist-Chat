Policy 340 — Body-Worn Cameras

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2024-03-01. Revision 2. Current revision.

340.1 Activation

Officers shall activate their body-worn cameras at the start of any enforcement contact. Cameras remain on until the contact has concluded. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.

340.2 Retention

Routine recordings are retained for 180 days. Recordings that document a use of force or an arrest are flagged and retained under the evidence retention schedule. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

340.3 Review Before Reporting

Officers may review their own recordings before writing a report, except following any in-custody death, where review is allowed only after the investigator's approval. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
