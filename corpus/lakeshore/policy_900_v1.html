Policy 900 — Public Records Requests

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2023-02-10. Revision 1. Current revision.

900.1 Response Time

Public records requests are fulfilled within 15 business days of receipt. Requests are logged by the records unit and acknowledged in writing. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

900.2 Copy Fees

Copies of public records are charged at $0.25 per page. Fees are waived for requests made by the subject of the record. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
