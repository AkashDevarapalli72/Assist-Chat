Policy 802 — Property and Evidence

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2021-06-15. Revision 1. SUPERSEDED on 2024-03-01.

802.1 Packaging

Evidence shall be packaged in sealed plastic evidence pouches and sealed with the collecting officer's initials and date. Biological evidence must be dried before packaging. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.

802.2 Submission Deadline

All collected evidence must be submitted to the property room within 14 days of collection. Items that cannot be submitted in time must be secured in a temporary locker. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

802.3 Chain of Custody

Each transfer of evidence is recorded on the Chain of Custody Form 12. Any gap in the record shall be reported to the property supervisor immediately. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
