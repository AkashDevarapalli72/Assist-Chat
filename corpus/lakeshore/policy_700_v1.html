Policy 700 — Report Writing

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2023-02-10. Revision 1. Current revision.

700.1 Report Deadlines

Incident reports must be completed within 24 hours of the incident. Reports shall be factual, objective, and written in the first person. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.

700.2 Report Approval

Supervisors approve or return incident reports within three business days. Returned reports shall be corrected and resubmitted promptly. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.
