Policy 210 — Emergency Vehicle Operation

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2025-01-06. Revision 1. Current revision.

210.1 Intersections

Officers operating in emergency mode shall stop at red signals and stop signs in controlled intersections unless cross-traffic is clearly stopped before proceeding. Emergency lights and siren must be activated for the duration of the response. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
