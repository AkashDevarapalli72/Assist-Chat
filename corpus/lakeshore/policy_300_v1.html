Policy 300 — Use of Force

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2023-02-10. Revision 1. Current revision.

300.1 Duty to Intervene

Any officer who observes another officer using force that is clearly beyond what is objectively reasonable shall intervene when it is safe to do so. The intervening officer shall notify a supervisor and document the intervention in writing within 8 hours. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

300.2 Use of Force Reporting

A use of force report is required whenever force beyond routine control holds is used. The involved officer submits the report before the end of shift, and a supervisor completes the review within 5 business days. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

300.3 Deadly Force Review

Every intentional discharge of a firearm at a person is referred for administrative review by the Force Review Panel. The review is independent of the chain of command of the involved officer. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.
