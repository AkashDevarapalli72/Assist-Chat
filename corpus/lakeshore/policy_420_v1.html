Policy 420 — Crisis Response

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2023-02-10. Revision 1. Current revision.

420.1 Crisis Intervention

Officers who encounter a person in mental health crisis shall request a co-responder clinician when one is available. Officers should use de-escalation and allow time before taking enforcement action. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

420.2 Involuntary Holds

An officer may initiate an involuntary evaluation hold lasting up to 48 hours when the person is an imminent danger to self or others. The hold paperwork must accompany the person to the facility. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
