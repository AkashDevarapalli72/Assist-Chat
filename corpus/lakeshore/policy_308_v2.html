Policy 308 — Conducted Energy Weapons

Lakeshore County Sheriff's Office. Effective 2024-03-01. Revision 2. Current revision.

308.1 Deployment Criteria

Conducted energy weapons (commonly called Tasers) may be deployed only against a person who is actively resisting or who poses an immediate threat of harm. Deployment against a person who is only fleeing is limited to felony suspects. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.

308.2 Cycle Limits

Officers shall not deliver more than five cycles on a single subject without reassessing whether the weapon is effective. Each cycle must be documented in the use of force report. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.

308.3 Medical Evaluation

Any person struck by a conducted energy weapon probe shall be evaluated by a physician at an emergency department before being booked. Probes embedded in sensitive areas are removed only by medical personnel. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.
