Policy 306 — Vehicle Pursuits

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2020-09-01. Revision 1. SUPERSEDED on 2023-11-20.

306.1 Authorization

Vehicle pursuits are authorized only when the suspect is wanted for any offense involving flight. Officers shall weigh the danger of the pursuit against the need for immediate apprehension. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

306.2 Number of Pursuit Units

No more than seven units may actively engage in a pursuit unless the supervisor authorizes additional vehicles. Additional units shall remain on parallel streets only when approved. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.

306.3 Pursuit Termination

The supervisor shall order a pursuit terminated when speeds exceed 25 mph over the posted limit in a residential zone. A terminated pursuit must be acknowledged on the radio by every involved unit. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.
