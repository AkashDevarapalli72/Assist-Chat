Policy 900 — Public Records Requests

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

900.1 Response Time

Public records requests are fulfilled within 20 business days of receipt. Requests are logged by the records unit and acknowledged in writing. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

900.2 Copy Fees

Copies of public records are charged at $0.15 per page. Fees are waived for requests made by the subject of the record. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.
