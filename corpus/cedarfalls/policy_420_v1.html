Policy 420 — Crisis Response

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

420.1 Crisis Intervention

Officers who encounter a person in mental health crisis shall request a mobile crisis unit when one is available. Officers should use de-escalation and allow time before taking enforcement action. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

420.2 Involuntary Holds

An officer may initiate an involuntary evaluation hold lasting up to 96 hours when the person is an imminent danger to self or others. The hold paperwork must accompany the person to the facility. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.
