Policy 330 — Foot Pursuits

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2022-05-01. Revision 1. Current revision.

330.1 Pursuing Alone

Officers shall not initiate a foot pursuit alone unless an imminent threat to others exists. Officers should consider containment and waiting for backup. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

330.2 Radio Broadcast

Officers engaged in a foot pursuit shall broadcast their location, direction of travel, and the number of suspects. Radio traffic is kept clear for the pursuing officer. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.
