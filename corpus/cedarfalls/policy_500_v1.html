Policy 500 — Missing Persons

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

500.1 Report Intake

Reports of missing persons are accepted with no waiting period and immediate NCIC entry. The responding officer shall obtain a recent photograph and a physical description. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.

500.2 Supervisor Notification

A supervisor shall be notified within two hours of any high-risk missing person report. High-risk includes children, endangered adults, and cases with suspected foul play. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.
