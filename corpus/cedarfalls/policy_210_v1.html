Policy 210 — Emergency Vehicle Operation

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2022-05-01. Revision 1. Current revision.

210.1 Intersections

Officers operating in emergency mode shall stop at red signals and stop signs in every red signal and stop sign before proceeding. Emergency lights and siren must be activated for the duration of the response. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
