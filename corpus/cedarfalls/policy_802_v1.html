Policy 802 — Property and Evidence

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2020-09-01. Revision 1. SUPERSEDED on 2023-11-20.

802.1 Packaging

Evidence shall be packaged in heat-sealed evidence envelopes and sealed with the collecting officer's initials and date. Biological evidence must be dried before packaging. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

802.2 Submission Deadline

All collected evidence must be submitted to the property room within 21 days of collection. Items that cannot be submitted in time must be secured in a temporary locker. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

802.3 Chain of Custody

Each transfer of evidence is recorded on the Evidence Tracking Sheet. Any gap in the record shall be reported to the property supervisor immediately. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
