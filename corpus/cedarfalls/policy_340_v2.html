Policy 340 — Body-Worn Cameras

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2023-11-20. Revision 2. Current revision.

340.1 Activation

Officers shall activate their body-worn cameras at arrival at any call involving a possible arrest. Cameras remain on until the contact has concluded. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

340.2 Retention

Routine recordings are retained for 365 days. Recordings that document a use of force or an arrest are flagged and retained under the evidence retention schedule. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.

340.3 Review Before Reporting

Officers may review their own recordings before writing a report, except following a critical incident involving serious injury, where review is allowed only after the investigator's approval. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.
