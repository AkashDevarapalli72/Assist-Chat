Policy 600 — Domestic Violence

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

600.1 Arrest Decisions

In domestic violence incidents officers shall use arrest of the primary aggressor when probable cause exists. The decision shall be documented regardless of whether an arrest is made. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

600.2 Supplemental Report

A domestic violence supplemental report is due within 24 hours. Officers shall also provide the victim with a resource card. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.
