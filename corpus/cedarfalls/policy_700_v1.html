Policy 700 — Report Writing

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

700.1 Report Deadlines

Incident reports must be completed within 16 hours of the incident. Reports shall be factual, objective, and written in the first person. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.

700.2 Report Approval

Supervisors approve or return incident reports within one business day. Returned reports shall be corrected and resubmitted promptly. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit.
