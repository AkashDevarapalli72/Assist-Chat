Policy 300 — Use of Force

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2024-08-20. Revision 1. Current revision.

300.1 Duty to Intervene

Any officer who observes another officer using force that is clearly beyond what is objectively reasonable shall intervene when it is safe to do so. The intervening officer shall notify a supervisor and document the intervention in writing within 12 hours. Questions about this section should be directed to the Policy Unit. Training on this section is provided annually.

300.2 Use of Force Reporting

A use of force report is required whenever force beyond routine control holds is used. The involved officer submits the report before the end of shift, and a supervisor completes the review within 7 business days. Training on this section is provided annually. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy.

300.3 Deadly Force Review

Every intentional discharge of a firearm at a person is referred for administrative review by the Executive Review Committee. The review is independent of the chain of command of the involved officer. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.
