Policy 308 — Conducted Energy Weapons

Cedar Falls Police Department. Effective 2020-09-01. Revision 1. SUPERSEDED on 2023-11-20.

308.1 Deployment Criteria

Conducted energy weapons (commonly called Tasers) may be deployed only against a person who is actively resisting or who poses an immediate threat of harm. Deployment against a person who is only fleeing is allowed only when the subject is armed. Violations may result in corrective action under the disciplinary policy. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff.

308.2 Cycle Limits

Officers shall not deliver more than twelve cycles on a single subject without reassessing whether the weapon is effective. Each cycle must be documented in the use of force report. This section applies to all sworn and professional staff. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section.

308.3 Medical Evaluation

Any person struck by a conducted energy weapon probe shall be evaluated by paramedics on scene before being booked. Probes embedded in sensitive areas are removed only by medical personnel. Supervisors are responsible for ensuring compliance with this section. Exceptions require written approval from the agency head or designee.
